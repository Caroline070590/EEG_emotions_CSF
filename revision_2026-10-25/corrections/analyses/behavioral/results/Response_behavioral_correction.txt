We corrected the outcome preprocessing in the supplementary leave-one-video-out prediction analysis. Each participant's outcome mean and standard deviation are now estimated from the 27 training videos and applied unchanged to the held-out video; all four models, including the participant-only reference, were refitted. Held-out outcome values do not enter scaling or model fitting. We report RMSE after returning predictions to original outcome units and provide training-standardized errors as a sensitivity analysis. No rating-only or combined versus assigned-label comparison survives correction in either scale (minimum BH-adjusted q=0.611 and 0.179, respectively). The updated table and prediction panel therefore preserve the conclusion without relying on held-out outcome information.
