Participant generalization and feature-group ranking — 24 October 2026

Exploratory extension requested after inspection of the existing video-held-out results.
This is not a preregistered or independent confirmation of the existing architectures.

Data: existing FACED 30-s trial-level feature cache, 123 participants x 28 videos.
Five fixed participant-disjoint folds; every participant contributes held-out
predictions exactly once. The same 28 videos occur in training and test participants.
This is five-fold participant holdout, not leave-one-participant-out or joint holdout.

Models: existing hybrid GNN with conditional video adversary, existing summary-first
GNN without adversary, and shrinkage LDA. All receive the same 5,520 scalar inputs:
channel-wise power and recurrence measures, band-specific wPLI/oAEC, signed Spearman
edges, and joint recurrence. MTF/QG state graphs and graph-summary topology metrics
are not inputs. Hyperparameters/40 epochs are inherited and fixed; no feature
selection or model choice will use held-out performance.

Preprocessing: load raw cached features, fit every scalar mean/standard deviation
on training participants only, apply to held-out participants; do not normalize
using the held-out participant's distribution. Training-constant features are zeroed.
This differs from the transductive within-participant scaling in the earlier
video-held-out evaluation, so changes in accuracy cannot be attributed to the split alone.

Primary training seed20261024; neural seeds11 and29 are sensitivity runs, reported
separately. LDA is deterministic. Balanced accuracy, participant-bootstrap95% intervals,
confusion matrices, and paired participant-bootstrap model differences will be reported.

Null: 99 permutations of the video-to-condition assignment, identical across all
participants and models, preserving the video counts of the nine categories. Refit
all training folds for each permutation using the primary seed. Familiar videos can
support above11.11% performance even with shuffled labels. Inferential p-values are
reported only after all99 runs per model; Holm adjustment across the three models.

Ranking: fixed fitted models, held-out participants only. Shuffle whole input blocks
across videos within each held-out participant, with an identical video reordering
across held-out participants. Twenty-two atomic feature types plus four broader
families, 30 shuffles each. Perturb the inputs to both graph and global-summary
branches. Report drop in balanced accuracy, participant-bootstrap intervals and
fold/rank stability. Negative estimates are retained. These measure model reliance
under perturbation, not causal mechanisms or unique information among correlated inputs.
