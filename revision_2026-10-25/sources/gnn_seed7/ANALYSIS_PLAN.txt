SEED-VII multi-view GNN extension — 24 October 2026

Exploratory extension requested after the FACED models and the earlier SEED-VII
scalar-feature analyses were inspected. This is a new dataset-specific evaluation
of the fixed modelling methodology; it is not a preregistered confirmation or
transfer of fitted FACED network weights to SEED-VII.

Data: the existing SEED-VII final-30-s cache, 20 participants x 80 clips, the same
30 channels, resampled from 200 to 250 Hz during the existing cache preparation.
Seven conditions: anger, disgust, fear, sadness, neutral, happiness, surprise.
No raw EEG modification or new feature tuning. Use all 5,520 scalar inputs:
five channel power bands, five channel recurrence measures, five wPLI and five
oAEC edge views, signed Spearman edges and joint recurrence edges.

Five participant-disjoint folds, fixed seed 20261024. Each participant is tested
once; every training fold contains all 80 clips and all four recording sessions.
Fit scalar means and standard deviations using training participants only.
The scope is new participants viewing familiar clips in familiar sessions.

Models: inherited hybrid GNN with conditional clip adversary (lambda=1),
summary-first GNN without adversary, and input-matched shrinkage LDA. The neural
architecture is unchanged except seven class outputs and 80 clip-adversary outputs.
Fixed inherited hyperparameters and 40 epochs. Primary training seed 20261024;
seeds 11 and 29 are separate neural sensitivity runs, not selected or ensembled.

Null: 99 global permutations of labels across the 56 source-by-condition blocks,
shared across participants and models, with complete refitting of all five folds.
Clips within a source-by-condition block stay together; block-class counts remain
fixed, while clip-class counts may change. Two sources have clips in two conditions
and therefore contribute two independently permutable blocks. This null does not
condition on session-by-condition counts. Retain all results. Report permutation
p-values only after all 99 runs, with Holm adjustment over the three EEG models.

Recording-session diagnostic: condition frequencies differ by session. Predict
condition from session identity alone using training-fold counts and the decision
maximizing training balanced accuracy. Report its score and paired participant
comparisons with the EEG models. This diagnostic is not a session-conditional null.

Feature rankings: all 22 atomic feature types and four broader families, using
30 held-out input-block shuffles per group. A shared clip reordering is applied
within each held-out participant. Perturb both graph and direct-summary branches.
Report balanced-accuracy drops, fold-rank stability and pointwise participant-
bootstrap intervals (2,000 draws). These quantify fitted-model reliance; they do
not establish unique or causal physiological contributions. Intervals condition
on the fitted models and the familiar clip/session design.

Report accuracy, model-specific null distributions, confusion matrices, matched
linear comparisons, seed sensitivity and rankings. The theoretical seven-class
chance level is 1/7, but familiar clip/session structure can support higher null
scores. New results enter manuscript prose only after verification.
